      ServiceNow MARZ Acronym & Metric Guide

      Plain-English definitions for every metric in this dashboard

      $ Marketing Influenced Pipeline (MTA — Multi-Touch Attribution)

      In enterprise B2B sales, buyers engage with multiple assets before purchasing. Instead of giving 100% of credit to a single click, Multi-Touch Attribution (MTA) shares dollar pipeline credit across every content asset that helped educate or accelerate the buying committee across the deal cycle.

      $ GSD Created Pipeline (Global Sales Development)

      GSD is ServiceNow's Global Sales Development team (SDRs / BDRs). This metric measures the dollar value of brand-new sales opportunities directly originated and booked by SDRs following up on leads from your content.

      # Stage 1 Optys vs. # Stage 2+ Optys (Sales Opportunities)

      Optys = Sales Opportunities in CRM. Stage 1 means a rep held an initial discovery call and opened an early potential deal. Stage 2+ means the deal passed strict qualification (confirmed budget, authority, need, timeline) and is accepted as official Sales Pipeline. High Stage 1 → Stage 2+ conversion proves your content attracts serious buyers.

      # Interactions & # Unique Leads

      Interactions = Total clicks, views, or downloads. Unique Leads = Deduplicated count of individual people who engaged.

      # Net New Interactions

      Engagements from brand-new people who were not previously active in ServiceNow's database (measures net-new logo reach).

      # MQLs (Marketing Qualified Leads)

      Leads whose content engagement and buyer profile scored high enough to send to the GSD (SDR) team for outreach.
